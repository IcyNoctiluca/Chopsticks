Rules (classic-v1)

  • Each player has two hands (L/R) starting at 1.
  • Tap: choose one of your living hands and add its value to a living target hand (your other hand or an opponent's hand).
  • A hand reaching 5 or more dies (becomes 0) and is out.
  • Split (revival only): if one of your hands is dead, redistribute your total across both hands; both must be 1+.
  • Win by making both of the opponent's hands dead.
